Quick Calc Pad

A notepad-style calculator on a web page: type calculations in plain language, line by line, and see the answers right away.

The unique kernel

Percentages written the way people say them, such as 20% off 80 or VAT 21% on 150, with results you can name and reuse on later lines. Templates turn common jobs like VAT into “load it and change the numbers.”

Who it's for

Anyone who needs a quick discount, VAT amount, or price with tax, without doing several steps on a calculator or asking an AI.

Why it matters to you

“Something people can use quickly on a website.” You also want to practice defining an MVP “so I don't keep adding features over and over and never ship.”

Core loop

Open, type, see answers. Or press a template, change the numbers, done. No account, nothing to set up.

Inspiration

Soulver: plain-language lines, answers in a column, lines that refer to each other. The feel: “enter and start typing.”

The boundary

Now In the proof of concept
  • Typing calculation lines with results shown right away: percentages, discounts, VAT, and general math (brackets, powers, and similar)
  • Naming results so later lines can reuse them
  • A VAT template button, plus a few more templates (such as discounts)
  • Saving the page in the browser so it's still there next time, with no account
Later Worth doing, not now
  • Currencies and anything else that needs live outside data, because they require an API.
Cut Deliberately left out
  • Accounts and logins: “no username no nothing, just enter and start typing.”
