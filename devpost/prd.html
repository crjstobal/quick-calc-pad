Quick Calc Pad

A notepad-style calculator for quick percentage, discount, VAT, and interest answers, with no calculator gymnastics and no AI.

What the PRD added to the scope

Scope said: “naming results so later lines can reuse them.”
→
Now we know: every line is named line1, line2… automatically. Click a name to rename it, and every line that uses it updates too.
Scope said: “a VAT button, plus a few more templates.”
→
Now we know: your own Notes tab plus three template tabs, VAT, Discounts, and Compound interest. Each is a separate sheet, and switching never loses anything.

Features and how we'll know they work

Typing calculations

Each line is calculated as you type. Supported: + - * /, brackets, powers, X% of Y, X% off Y, Y + X%, Y - X%.

  • 20% off 80 shows 64
  • (2 + 3) * 4 shows 20
  • 150 + 21% shows 181.5
  • Results update with no button press
  • Empty lines show no result and no error
Line names and reuse
  • line1 * 2 doubles line 1
  • Renaming line1 to price updates every line that uses it, and results stay the same
  • Changing line 1 updates everything that depends on it
Errors and fixes
  • pric + 5 shows “I don't know pric. Did you mean price?”, and clicking fixes it
  • One broken line doesn't stop the others
Templates (folder tabs)
  • VAT shows a sheet with net, rate, VAT amount, and total
  • Going back to Notes shows your lines untouched
  • Changing the net updates the VAT amount and total
  • Discounts and Compound interest each load their own working example
Saving in the browser
  • Reloading restores every tab, including template edits

States

  • First visit: intro tips visible, empty typing area, ready to type.
  • Line with an error: marked with a tooltip, while other lines keep working.
  • Returning visit: every tab is restored, and the page opens on Notes.

Boundary

Deferred Not in this version
  • Currencies and other live data (needs an API)
  • Fix suggestions for errors other than misspelled names
  • Units and dates
Non-goals Won't do
  • Accounts or logins
  • An AI assistant
  • Long documents
